*dreams, reflections, and the patterns between*

### What is this project?
Over the first half of 2026, I went through a massive internal shift. It was a fascinating experience to live through and, perhaps because this shift was triggered by [[Realization & Shock|interpreting an old childhood dream]], I followed its progression in real time by paying special attention to my dream content for about seven months. The result is that I now have a sprawling collection of dreams and reflective writings – both from the dates given on the dream recollections and from roughly "now" as they are posted here – that essentially tell the story of what I've been referring to privately as my "reintegration phase": a coming home to myself. It's part memoir, part journal, and part observational notes.

Rather than presenting my full collection of dreams and reflections all at once, I'm opting to share them in sections as I revisit and complete them, at my own leisure. There is no set timeline to completion, but I do feel confident that they will all be here to read, eventually. This site itself is also likely to evolve in terms of functionality and content over time. If you would like to be notified when new instalments are posted, you can sign up for my mailing list.

> [!hint]- Acknowledgements
> Before sharing, I want to acknowledge the influences and resources I leaned on through this process. Though ultimately self-guided, my process was given a speed boost through a combination of two sets of guiding tools:
> 1. **The dream interpretation framework provided by dream analyst [Jane Teresa Anderson](https://www.janeteresa.com/)**, whose long-running podcast, The Dream Show, first grabbed my interest in 2023. Jane Teresa has [a set of self-paced online courses](https://www.janeteresa.com/online-dream-interpretation-and-dream-therapy-courses/) where she shares her process, and it is from listening to her podcast and completing the first two of her courses that I have learned to recognise the particular way my own dreams reflect the landscape of my inner world.
> 2. **A [custom GPT trained on Human Design](https://thewildpixel.com/human-design-chatgpt-ai) by self-styled "Human Design Goblin" [Fiona Wong](https://thewildpixel.com/)**, a tool I picked up on a curious and somewhat skeptical whim when she offered it in late 2025 for a very low price. It immediately showed its value to me as a way to explore nuanced questions about [[Human Design/|Human Design]], a system whose mechanics I've been learning, which would have been *extremely* difficult to answer deeply anywhere else. Having access to this GPT feels a bit like talking to myself (which is how I process a lot of ideas), only that other "self" is steeped in HD knowledge. It can provide a perspective on any scenario I might wish to explore through the lens of [[My Human Design Chart|my own design]] in a way that deepens not only my understanding of the system itself, but my appreciation for my own unique experience of life.
> 
> It was an experimental combination of these two things – [[Realization & Shock|practicing my interpretation skills on a childhood dream through a lens of Human Design within the container of the GPT]] – that made something click for me. My interpretation (which at that point relied more rigidly on reading the symbolism than the more intuitive approach I'm drawn to now) showed me something that Human Design knowledge had given me a secondary way to describe, and that description floored me. That moment was the entry point to the entire "reintegration" process, and it's hard to say when I might have naturally reached this point without the help of either influence: I am beyond grateful for this intersection of concepts and for the people who made this intersection possible for me.

> [!warning]- A couple small disclaimers
> ##### Regarding AI-generated content:
> Although I did explore most of these dreams together with the Human Design Companion AI soon after I'd had them (this was particularly helpful in keeping me grounded through the earliest and most distressing dreams), those conversations served as a both an interactive learning experience and a reflective jumping point for me, and I've chosen to keep them private. **Everything I share in my reflections has been fully internalized and written much later by me:** if I choose to share direct excerpts from my chat with the GPT, I will label them as such.
> 
> ##### For anyone reading who knows me personally:
> Though many of the characters in these dreams are entirely made up, there is a chance you will have appeared as a character in a dream of mine, especially if we are related. For the sake of interpretation, I feel it's important to provide waking-life relational context to my dream characters, so I do make frequent use of familial terms; however, I do my best to keep names out of my recollections. If I do feel a need to use names for clarity, I will choose a pseudonym. If you recognize yourself in a recollection, try not to read too deeply into your presence! All dream characters are highly symbolic, representing parts of myself or sometimes larger archetypes, and are *not* accurate representations of the real people they may appear to be.

### The different flavours of writing you'll find here:

> [!quote] **[[Dreams/|Dreams]]**
> The core material I'm sharing here is a selection of chronological dream recollections from between December 2025 and June 2026. They are lightly edited versions of the original recollections (written in my phone before getting out of bed in the mornings, a practice I've had since 2023). This project showcases the dreams that I felt were particularly reflective of my inner landscape at that time and which I also feel comfortable sharing: it is by no means a full account of all the dreams I had during this period or even of all the dreams I looked at deeply.

> [!abstract] Companion Reflections
> Alongside each dream is a reflection on my interpretation of that dream, sometimes referencing previous dreams or emerging patterns I may have noticed in hindsight. I've nested these types of reflections in foldable "callouts": just tap the arrow to read my thoughts, or leave these closed to practice your own interpretation skills, if you like.

> [!NOTE] Standalone Reflections
> Sometimes I feel called to make an edit or inject a standalone reflection in a more typical "blog post" fashion that might end up bridging different sections together, which is one reason why the name **Living Threads** does a good job of describing this project: the way these dreams and reflections relate back to each other feels very much alive, and to be able to leave loose ends or weave in new pieces as they naturally connect over time is such a joy.

> [!example] **[[Human Design/|Human Design Geekery]]**
> Since my interest in Human Design played a big part in contextualizing the [[Hot Dog Body|initial childhood dream]] that sparked the shift this project follows, it's also a big part of the way I felt drawn to contextualize the shift itself. In fact, in Human Design terms, much of this journey can be categorized as an example of "deconditioning." Without fail, when I try to include a *little* bit of HD info to share these insights in my reflections, it ends up turning into a **lot**. I've decided to embrace it: when I have something HD-related to say inside my companion reflections, a link to a more full post will be included at the bottom of the reflection. If you'd like to read [[Human Design/|a more general primer on Human Design]] or take a look at [[My Human Design Chart|my chart]], you can do that too.

### Not sure where to start?
While I encourage exploring the nested folder system in the sidebar at your leisure, the heart of this project is the progression of dream content I've recorded.

The dream explorations here have been laid out in chronological stages. It all started with an interpretation of a childhood dream, so **the best entry point is to dive in at the beginning with a stage I've named [[Realization & Shock]]**. This page contains:
- the original childhood dream and my interpretation of that dream from December 2025
- a reflection on the waking-life childhood experience I feel the dream may have been addressing
- the steps I took after gaining insight about the long-term impact of that childhood experience
- the shock response dream I had one day after interpreting the childhood dream and a reflection on that dream

If reading through dreams in the full stage page is overwhelming to look at, **you can read through each posted dream individually, starting with [[Hot Dog Body]]**. There will be links at the bottom of the pages to the previous and next dreams in the progression. However, reading in this way may result in skipping past other relevant linked sections and posts, as the stage pages also serve as hubs for other reflections on the internal questions I was working through during that same time period.

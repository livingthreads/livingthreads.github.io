# Discovering Human Design: a disconnect from my own design
I was drawn to learn about [[My Human Design Chart#My Chart|my own chart]] because the recognition in the descriptions I was reading alongside mine was precise, but not *complete* for me. There was a tantalizing feeling of the chart touching *something* real, but still feeling obscured. I began learning with the hope that deeper understanding would fill in the gaps for me, but instead I fell into learning for its own sake (which, incidentally, is natural for my design).

This change in approach – from searching for answers to simply exploring my curiosities – led me to all sorts of places, one of which was an experiment of combining my growing Human Design knowledge with my established interest in dream interpretation. It was here where I stumbled onto the very path that I'd been searching for earlier: my grasp of dream symbolism and emotional texture, applied to a childhood dream at what felt like random, was suddenly equipped with Human Design language and revealed to me [[HD Themes in my Childhood Dream#The Focal Point 10–20 Channel of Awakening|a pinpointed distortion in the lived expression of my chart]] that accounted for everything I'd found foreign and unrelatable on my initial reading.

---
## The parts of [[My Human Design Chart#My Chart|my chart]] that felt hard to relate to:
### Splenic Authority
"Authority" in Human Design describes the way a person's system is wired to make decisions. Splenic authority is one of the more mysterious and difficult to grasp, described as "quietly saying 'no' once before going silent" with many stories of people only learning to identify its signal retrospectively after they've ignored it and subsequently felt regret. Because it only really applies to in-the-moment decisions and is inherently intuitive vs. conventionally logical, it's very common for people with this authority to learn to steamroll over it with logic and practicality (which is absolutely a familiar description for me).

### Line 3 Profile
My profile is 3/6, commonly called "The Martyr/Role Model" profile. Line 3 is about hands-on experimentation with life, learning what works and what doesn't through experience. Line 6 has a unique trajectory which mirrors Line 3 in the first 30 years of life (leading people with a 3/6 profile to be described as having a usually chaotic early life) before going "on the roof" until age 50 (a more observational and even withdrawn phase, and the phase I'm in now), eventually coming "off the roof" after midlife and embodying the lessons they've learned from their early life in a way that others tend to look up to. I felt a bit defeated on learning I was a Line 3 because I could relate to endlessly trying and failing, but I also felt confused about the common descriptions of Line 3 and the way they read as adventurous, curious, and always ready to explore, especially during early life.

### 10–20 Channel
One of the two channels present in my chart is 10–20 "The Channel of Awakening." This channel in particular was one of the aspects of my chart that I felt a disconnect from when starting my learning: while it carries a theme of having a strong connection to the cultivation of self-love (a topic which I do feel has become core to me for several years now), that self-love is expressed through what is most succinctly described as a wiring to be myself in the moment without justification, which felt *extremely* hard to relate to for pretty much as far back as I could remember.

---

At face value, without knowing anything about my own lived experience, these parts of my chart read as someone who is:
- wired to participate in aligned experiences without a second thought
- capable of quickly detecting misaligned or poorly timed opportunities; not wasting energy on things that don't matter
- naturally inclined to experiment with ideas rather than simply intellectualize them

In short: someone who is more spontaneous and less concerned with outcome than I felt I was. I felt better characterised by caution and overthinking and inhibition, but my chart looked like someone whose life felt *uncomplicated* to live.

Now that I have a deeper understanding of chart mechanics in general, I can see a lot of clues as to why acting authentically and spontaneously may have felt socially challenging for me growing up. I actually think that on some level I was always going to take my time arriving at mental ease rather than living it from the start, but [[Hot Dog Body|the dream I interpreted]] showed me such a particular origin of distortion to my 10–20 channel that it was clear to me on reflection exactly how far-reaching that distortion went, and how detached from the flow of my design everything after really became.
